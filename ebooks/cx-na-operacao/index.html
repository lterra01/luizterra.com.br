INSIDE THE EBOOK

Seven routines. One follow-up cycle.

  1. 01

    Understand the reason for contact

    Separate the channel from the customer need that must be completed.

  2. 02

    Preserve context

    Structure case handoffs around useful history, the next action and an owner.

  3. 03

    Investigate repeat contacts

    Distinguish a new need from a return about the same unresolved problem.

  4. 04

    Escalate voice incidents

    Record impact and useful evidence for the responsible technical team.

  5. 05

    Deliver the next step

    Connect promises to an action, an owner, a deadline and verification.

  6. 06

    Align BPOs and suppliers

    Make responsibilities and escalation paths explicit.

  7. 07

    Review evidence and decide

    Use operational reviews to decide what to change and verify the result.
